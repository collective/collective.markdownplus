Third-party bundled assets
==========================

This distribution bundles minified third-party frontend assets under
src/collective/markdownplus/browser/resources/vendor.

The project itself is licensed under MIT as stated above. Bundled third-party
assets remain under their own licenses from their original projects.

Bundled projects:

1. EasyMDE (includes CodeMirror and related bundled dependencies)
	- Path: src/collective/markdownplus/browser/resources/vendor/easymde/
	- Upstream: https://github.com/Ionaru/easy-markdown-editor
	- License in distributed asset header: MIT

2. KaTeX (JS, CSS, auto-render, and font files)
	- Path: src/collective/markdownplus/browser/resources/vendor/katex/
	- Upstream: https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX
	- Upstream license: MIT

3. Mermaid
	- Path: src/collective/markdownplus/browser/resources/vendor/mermaid/
	- Upstream: https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid
	- Upstream license: MIT

When redistributing this package, preserve this LICENSE.txt and ensure
compliance with the upstream license terms of bundled third-party assets.
